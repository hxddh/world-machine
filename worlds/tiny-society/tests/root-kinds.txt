# The kinds of event that begin a chain of their own: nothing recorded
# caused them (AGENTS.md invariant 5). Every other event this Pack records
# names its cause, and roots are under a tenth of all events
# (tests/invariants.rs, world-pack-testkit/src/invariants.rs). A saved
# World's events recorded before v0.29 keep their old form and are not
# checked. Keep this list short: a new kind of event says what caused it.

# The World beginning.
arrived              # the player arrives in a new harbour

# A day passing (the calendar System): everything the day's rules do
# follows from it.
day_began

# The player's own deeds. What comes of each follows from it; an answer
# follows the question it answers, so answers are not listed here.
built_by_hand        # making something with their own hands
decorated_by_hand
planted_by_hand
moved_by_hand
undone_by_hand       # taking back what they just did
designed             # painting a design on something
named                # giving something a name
spoken               # saying something in their own words
suggestion_held      # suggesting something to do
guest_visited        # a guest from another of the player's Worlds
